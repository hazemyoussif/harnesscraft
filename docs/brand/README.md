# HarnessCraft brand identity

HarnessCraft uses the **Scarab Circuit** identity: a modern Egyptian-inspired scarab fused with circuit geometry.

The identity is intentionally subtle. Ancient Egyptian influence appears through symmetry, the scarab silhouette, wing geometry, and the solar orb. Product UI, typography, and layouts remain modern developer-tool design rather than historical ornament.

## Meaning

- **Scarab** — protection, renewal, persistence.
- **Wings** — reach, enablement, and leverage.
- **Circuit paths** — engineering systems and machine intelligence.
- **Central vertical path** — judgment and control staying at the center of the system.
- **Solar orb** — clarity and direction.

- **Sora** — brand moments, headings, UI titles.
- **Inter** — body text, documentation, product UI.
- Monospace remains the user's terminal/editor choice; HarnessCraft should not force a coding font.

## Usage rules

1. Prefer dark backgrounds and purple/lavender accents.
2. Keep the scarab symmetric and recognizable at small sizes.
3. Do not replace the scarab with a generic monogram in terminal or developer-facing surfaces.
4. Avoid hieroglyphic decoration as background noise; Egyptian influence should feel structural, not costume-like.
5. Preserve generous negative space around the mark.
6. Monochrome white is the preferred fallback when gradients are unavailable.
